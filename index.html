04About us

Fresh veg food, made for sharing

Vrindhavan is a casual vegetarian restaurant in Al Rigga serving North and South Indian classics — from crackling dosas and comfort thalis to evening chaat and hot chai. Counter and table service, quick plates, honest prices.

Bring the family, bring the office, bring a big appetite. Groups are always welcome, and there's a special South Indian menu every Friday.
